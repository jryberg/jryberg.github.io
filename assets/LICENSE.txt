2D assets in lemmings/ (used by the Lemmings X11 game)
=======================================================

MIT by Carlota Catot Bragós (https://github.com/carlotacb/Lemmings),
Copyright (c) 2018 Carlota Catot Bragós, MIT license.
  levels/<key>/map.png   terrain texture recreations of the iconic
                         Fun/Tricky/Taxing maps from the original game
  lemming_anim.png       lemming skill animation sheet
  menu/                  logo.png, menuBackground.png, modeSpriteSheet.png,
                         player1Sprite.png (main-menu art)
  ui/                    work_names.png (skill name labels), nums.png,
                         green_nums.png, lemm_font.png, reserved_words.png,
                         cursor.png, buttons.png (skill/tool buttons),
                         white_frame.png, black_frame.png, skull.png
  doors/                 lemming_doors.png, lemming_trapdoors.png
                         (exit door / entrance trapdoor animations)
  results/               perfect.png, good.png, justInTick.png, disaster.png,
                         buttons.png (post-level result screens)

NOTE: sfx/ contains the original Lemmings sound-effect WAV rips
(DMA Design) as distributed in the carlotacb/Lemmings repository;
included at the site owner's request.

NOTE: music/ contains seven original Lemmings music tracks
(MenuSong, InstructionsSong, Lemming1, Lemming2, Lemming3, Lemming4,
Lemming7 .ogg), also distributed in the carlotacb/Lemmings repository;
included at the site owner's request.
